# Content Index

## Pipeline

- [[05-Content/Ideas/Ideas Index|Ideas]] — angles, questions, hooks, and evidence gaps.
- [[05-Content/Scripts/Scripts Index|Scripts]] — drafts and production-ready scripts.
- [[05-Content/Published/Published Index|Published]] — released work and performance notes.

## Workflow

1. Capture an idea with a clear audience and problem.
2. Link supporting research and identify missing evidence.
3. Create a content brief with [[90-Templates/Content Brief Template]].
4. Draft in `Scripts`.
5. After release, move the final version to `Published` and record URL, date, platform, and lessons.

Platform-specific folders are intentionally not created until channel choices are confirmed in [[98-AI-Context/Platform Strategy]].
